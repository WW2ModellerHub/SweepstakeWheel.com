Simple Secret Santa rules for a fair gift exchange

Agree the basics before drawing so nobody is surprised by the practical details. Usually every participant buys one gift for the person they draw and receives one gift from somebody else.

  1. Confirm everyone is entered once.
  2. Agree a gift budget and exchange date.
  3. Set any theme or gift restrictions.
  4. Keep every assignment private.
  5. Redraw the whole group if somebody joins or leaves.
  6. Share only each person’s own recipient.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is this Secret Santa generator free?

Yes. The name draw, private pass-around mode and organiser list are free. You do not need an account or email address.

How do I draw Secret Santa names online?

Add each participant once, choose pass-around or organiser mode, then create the random draw. Everyone is assigned one different person and every name is used exactly once.

Can someone draw themselves?

No. The generator only accepts a draw when every participant has a different recipient, so there are no self-matches.

Can I use this Secret Santa generator for work?

Yes. Organiser mode creates one private list showing who buys for whom, ready for the organiser to send each colleague their own match.

What is private pass-around mode?

Pass-around mode is for a group in the same room. Each person privately reveals their own recipient, hides the name, then passes the device to the next participant.

Does the generator email everyone?

No. No email addresses are needed. Use pass-around mode in person, or use organiser mode and privately send each person their match yourself.

Are the names and matches private?

Names and matches stay in this browser tab and are not included in analytics. Organiser CSV and print files are created locally on your device.
