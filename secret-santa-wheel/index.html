FAQ

Quick answers

Is the Secret Santa generator free?

Yes. Both the private pass-around draw and the organiser list are free.

Can somebody draw themselves?

No. Every person receives one different recipient and every recipient is assigned exactly once.

Which Secret Santa mode should I use?

Choose pass-around when the group is together and each person can reveal their own match. Choose organiser when one person needs the full list showing who buys for whom for work or a remote group.

Are the names and matches private?

They stay in this browser tab and are not included in analytics. Organiser CSV and print files are created locally on your device.

Does the site save or email assignments?

No names or assignments are uploaded, stored or emailed. In organiser mode, download or print the private list and send each person only their own match.
