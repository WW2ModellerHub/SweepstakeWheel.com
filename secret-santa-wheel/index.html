FAQ

Quick answers

Is the Secret Santa generator free?

Yes. Creating and privately revealing every match is free.

Can somebody draw themselves?

No. Every person receives one different recipient and every recipient is assigned exactly once.

Are the names and matches private?

They stay in this browser tab. Only one recipient is shown at a time, and the name is removed before the next person’s turn.

Does the site save or email assignments?

No. Nothing is uploaded, stored or emailed. Keep the tab open until everybody has viewed their match.
