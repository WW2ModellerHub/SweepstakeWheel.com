FAQ

Quick answers

Can I create a custom sweepstake?

Yes. Choose the custom generator, add your own names and add your own options.

Is the wheel free?

Yes. You can run the draw and see the results for free. The export pack is a paid extra.

What if I have more people than teams?

The tool will reuse options so people can still play. If a doubled-up team wins, split the prize pot between those entrants.

Can I use this for work?

Yes. It is built for office sweepstakes, charity sweepstakes, sports draws and quick group games.
