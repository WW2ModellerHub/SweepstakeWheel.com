Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Sweepstake Wheel.

Draw information

Entrant names, options and results are processed by JavaScript in your browser. Sweepstake Wheel does not require an account and does not intentionally transmit your draw list to its own server. The free CSV is generated locally on your device.

Analytics and advertising

The site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage and Google AdSense to fund the free service. Google may process device, browser, IP address, interaction and advertising information and may use cookies or similar technologies according to its policies and your consent choices.

Amazon affiliate links

Links labelled as Amazon recommendations use an Associates tracking tag. Amazon may receive information when you follow a link and may set its own cookies. Purchases and account activity take place on Amazon, not Sweepstake Wheel.

Contact email

If you email hello@sweepstakewheel.com, the information you send is used to respond and maintain the site. Do not send sensitive personal or financial information.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser, use advertising controls provided by Google and avoid affiliate links. Blocking some scripts may affect analytics, advertising or site functionality. Privacy questions can be sent to the contact email above.
