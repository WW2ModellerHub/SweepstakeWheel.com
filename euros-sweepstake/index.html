FAQ

Quick answers

Is Sweepstake Wheel free?

Yes. The draw, results and CSV download are free to use.

How do I run a Euros sweepstake?

Add the entrants, check the competing nations, choose an allocation mode and start the draw. You can then spin one at a time or draw automatically.

What if I have more people than options?

The default mode uses every unique option once and shares them as evenly as possible; if options are fewer than people, some entrants stay unmatched. One each requires at least as many options as people and leaves any extra options undrawn.

Can I edit and download the results?

You can edit the names and options before drawing, then download the completed assignments as a free CSV file.
