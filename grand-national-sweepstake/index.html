Practical guide

How to run a Grand National sweepstake

Wait for the declared runners, agree the prize rules and complete the draw before the race begins.

1

Set up the draw

Wait for the declared runners, paste the horse names and draw as close to race day as practical. Larger groups can use more than one horse per person.

2

Agree fair rules

Agree the treatment of non-runners and whether prizes cover first place only or several finishing positions before collecting entries.

3

Share the result

Keep a copy of the assignments and declared runners. If a horse is withdrawn, use the rule agreed before the draw rather than redrawing selectively.

Quick checklist

  1. Confirm the entrant and option lists.
  2. Write down the prize and redraw rules.
  3. Load the draw and assign every entrant.
  4. Download the free results CSV and share it with the group.

Quick answers

Is Sweepstake Wheel free?

Yes. The draw, results and CSV download are free to use.

How do I run a Grand National sweepstake?

Add the entrants, paste the declared runners, load the draw and randomly assign the horses before the race.

What if I have more people than options?

The generator reuses shuffled options so everyone can still take part. Agree how duplicated winning options will share any prize before the draw.

Can I edit and download the results?

You can edit the names and options before drawing, then download the completed assignments as a free CSV file.
