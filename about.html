About Sweepstake Wheel

Sweepstake Wheel is a free browser-based tool for creating random assignments for sports tournaments, TV events, parties, charity activities and custom group games.

What the site does

Choose a template or add your own entrants and options. You can spin the wheel one person at a time, draw everyone automatically and download the completed assignments as a free CSV. Names and draw data are processed in your browser.

Fair, practical draws

The tool uses random selections in the browser. Organisers should agree entry fees, prizes, duplicated options, withdrawals and redraw rules before starting. The site does not collect stakes, hold prize money or operate betting services.

Content and updates

Event pages provide practical setup guidance and editable example lists. Tournament fields, contestants and runners can change, so organisers should check the relevant official event source before running a live draw. Corrections are welcome through the contact page.

How the free site is funded

Sweepstake Wheel is supported by advertising and clearly labelled Amazon affiliate links. Recommendations are selected for relevance to organising draws and watch parties; they do not affect the random result. See the affiliate disclosure for details.
